Why it's built this way

notes from one learner

Textbook audio lies

Studio dialogues are read, not spoken. Real Mandarin swallows syllables, drops tones under stress and runs words together — so every clip here comes from unscripted speech.

Words live in sentences

A word list gives you a label. Seeing the same word across a dozen real sentences gives you its shape, its register and the company it keeps.

Recall beats recognition

Reading a translation feels like learning. Rebuilding the sentence token by token is what actually moves it into memory — that's why assembly is the core loop, not a bonus game.

Everything stays on your device

No account, no server, no tracking. Your collections live in this browser — which is exactly why the backup export exists. Save the JSON often.
